# Territory Capture Plan\n\nA new feature inside the Rewards tab where users claim ~500m hexagons on a map of Hong Kong or Taiwan by running through them. Last runner to cross a hex owns it.\n\n## Scope\n\n- Lives entirely inside `RewardsTab` as a new third sub-tab: **Rewards | Leaderboards | Territory**.\n- No changes to any other tab, hook, or existing component.\n- Sources: Strava, Apple Health, Garmin, Terra activities (all already in DB with `summary_polyline`).\n\n## User experience\n\n1. User opens Rewards → Territory.\n2. Region toggle at top: **Hong Kong / Taiwan** (defaults to whichever region most of their recent runs fall in; remembered in localStorage).\n3. Leaflet map (reuse same tile provider as `ActivityMap.tsx`) bounded to the selected region.\n4. Hexagons rendered as colored polygons:\n   - **Yours** — primary accent fill\n   - **Someone else's** — muted gray fill\n   - **Unclaimed** — not drawn\n5. Tapping a hex shows a small popup: owner display name, captured date, total claims-history count.\n6. Stats strip above the map: `Hexes owned · Total ever captured · Rank in region`.\n7. \
